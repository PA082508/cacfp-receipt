How to Use This App
Step 1 — Who Are You?
🏫
Director — select your center, then select your name from the list.
Step 2 — Payment Method
💳
Corporate Card — Play Academy card. Automatically matched with bank statement. No extra fields needed.
💵
Cash — enter the amount and select the director who approved the purchase.
👤
Personal Card — enter the amount. Reimbursement will be processed automatically.
What to Photograph
🧾
Receipts & Invoices — store receipt, supplier invoice, card payment slip, or PDF document.
🏷️
Product Labels & Packaging — nutrition label, price tag, UPC barcode, case label with item number and pack size, shipping label. Used for inventory tracking and food cost records.
📄
Multiple photos OK — for long receipts, multi-page invoices, or several sides of a package.
AI automatically identifies: vendor · amounts · food vs non-food · milk gallons · product details from labels and barcodes.
Original Documents
📨
After uploading, place the original receipts or invoices in an envelope and give them to the food delivery driver to bring to the office.
Tips
✅
Make sure the document is fully visible and in focus before submitting.
🔄
Wrong file? Tap × on the thumbnail to remove it.
⬅️
Wrong name? Tap ← Back to go back and correct it.
👤
Name not in the list? Contact your director — staff list is managed by the administrator.
After Submission
☁️
Files are saved to secure cloud storage and processed automatically by AI.
💵
Cash purchases await manual director approval.
👤
Personal card purchases are added to the reimbursement queue.
